# Kleit Community Guidelines

**DRAFT v0.1 — 2026-09-21 — for the owner's review (spec 13.5, 17.2 #3). Not yet in force.**

Kleit is for making outfits and asking your friends what they think. Keep it kind, keep it PG-13, and keep it about the clothes.

## 1. Outfits, not people
Polls compare outfits. **Don't rate people's bodies**, and don't post selfies or photos of people's bodies into polls. No "hot or not".

## 2. Never allowed
1. **Child safety:** anything that sexualises a minor. Zero tolerance — the account is banned immediately and we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.
2. **Intimate images** of anyone shared without their consent, and nudity. Banned immediately. You can ask us to remove an intimate image of you without an account, and we remove it and identical copies within 48 hours.
3. **Threats** of violence. Banned immediately.

## 3. Not allowed
1. Harassment, bullying, or hate against people for who they are.
2. **Body-shaming**, and content that promotes eating disorders or self-harm. If you're struggling, the National Alliance for Eating Disorders and 988 can help.
3. Impersonating a person or a brand.
4. Photos of other people posted without their consent ("this is me" reports are taken seriously).
5. Spam, bots, and **bought or fake** likes, votes, follows or comments; rigging polls.
6. Phone numbers or street addresses in captions.
7. Usernames, captions or sets that aren't PG-13.
8. Counterfeits: you can clip them, but they get no Buy button, and you can report them.
9. Someone else's copyrighted work you don't have the right to use (see the Copyright Policy).

## 4. What happens when something breaks the rules
1. We remove it and tell you why, in plain words.
2. **Strikes:** three community strikes means your account is removed. Child-safety violations, non-consensual intimate images and threats mean an immediate ban. Copyright strikes are counted separately (see the Copyright Policy).
3. You can **appeal** any decision in the app, with one tap. A different person reviews it within 7 days.
4. Banned people may not come back with a new account.

## 5. Report anything
Every set, item, comment, profile, message and group has a **Report** button. You'll get a reference number and can see where your report got to. Reporting is anonymous to the person you report.
