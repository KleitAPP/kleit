# Kleit Copyright and DMCA Policy

**DRAFT v0.1 — 2026-09-21 — for the owner's review and the flat-fee lawyer review (spec 17.2 #4, 17.3). Not yet in force. Not legal advice.** The designated agent is registered with the U.S. Copyright Office after [Company] is formed (spec 17.3 #1).

## 1. We respect copyright
Kleit lets people save pictures of clothing from stores and other websites into their own sets. Each saved picture keeps a **locked link to the page it came from**. We keep **one stored copy** of each saved picture, tied to the person who saved it, and we check every picture automatically before anyone else sees it. We honour websites' "don't clip" tags, and brands and photographers can ask us to stop clipping from their site at [opt-out form].

## 2. Sending a takedown notice
If you believe something on Kleit infringes your copyright, send our designated agent a notice (no account needed — use the form at [kleit.app/copyright] or email [dmca email]) that includes:
1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. identification of the copyrighted work;
3. the link(s) on Kleit to the material you want removed;
4. your name, address, phone number and email;
5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use isn't authorised by the owner, its agent or the law;
6. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.

Designated agent: [name], [Company], [address], [dmca email]. We act on valid notices promptly and give you a reference number. We remove the picture and identical copies, and we replace it with a placeholder on every set that used it.

## 3. Counter-notices
If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification, you can send a counter-notice with: your signature; identification of the removed material and where it was; a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief it was removed by mistake or misidentification; your name, address and phone number; and consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, outside the U.S., any district where we may be found) and to accept service from the person who sent the notice. Unless the person who sent the notice tells us they've filed a lawsuit, we restore the material **10 to 14 business days** after receiving your counter-notice.

## 4. Repeat infringers
1. A valid takedown of something you uploaded or clipped is **one copyright strike** (several items in one notice count as one).
2. Strikes 1 and 2: the content is removed and you get an in-app and email warning.
3. **Strike 3 within 90 days: your account is removed** and you may not sign up again.
4. Strikes expire after 90 days, and a strike is removed if the notice is withdrawn or a counter-notice restores the content. Incomplete or bogus notices don't count.
5. Before an account is removed, its owner gets **14 days to download** their own non-infringing data.

## 5. Misuse
Knowingly false notices or counter-notices can make the sender liable for damages under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

## 6. Our own artwork
Kleit's stickers, frames and backgrounds are public-domain museum photographs (CC0) and our own drawings. If you believe one of them is yours, tell us at [dmca email] and we'll pull it while we look.
